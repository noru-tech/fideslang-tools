# W003: empty privacy declaration

**Severity:** warning · **Checked by:** [`src/validate/rules/hygiene.rs`](../../src/validate/rules/hygiene.rs)

**Rule:** every privacy declaration of a system should list at least one data category and at least
one data subject.

## What it checks

For each entry in a system's `privacy_declarations`, `fl` warns once if `data_categories` is missing,
`null` or empty, and once if `data_subjects` is. The `data_use` itself is required and checked by
[E007](E007.md).

## Why it matters

A privacy declaration says that a system processes some categories of data about some data subjects
for one data use. Without categories or subjects it states a purpose with nothing attached to it, so
policy evaluation and anything built from the declarations has nothing to check.

## Controls

A privacy declaration maps closely to an entry in a record of processing activities: GDPR
Art. 30(1)(b) and (c) ask for the purpose and for the categories of data subjects and of personal
data. An empty declaration leaves those parts of the entry blank.

## How to fix

List the categories of data the system processes for this use and whose data it is
(`fl taxonomy list subjects` shows the subjects). If the system processes no personal data for this
use, consider whether the declaration is needed at all.

## Suppress or promote

`--deny W003` (or `-W`) makes it fail the run. `--allow W003` drops it.
